CurvedLine
==========

Kotlin / Jetpack Compose app with [OpenMapView](https://github.com/afarber/OpenMapView), which draws curved lines between two draggable markers: a geodesic polyline along the great circle, as with `PolylineOptions.geodesic(true)` of the Google Maps API, and a polyline through the computed points of a Bezier arc.

Answer to the Stack Overflow question [How to draw a curved line between two coordinates using Osmdroid library](https://stackoverflow.com/q/54502178).
